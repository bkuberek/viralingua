# Viralingua

A free, local-first, cross-platform language-learning app with an AI tutor. Phone first, with a desktop version to follow.

You choose what to learn. The tutor designs a program around your goals and your level, or around content you bring. At the end of each stage it grades you and plans the next one.

## How it will work

- **Your progress stays on your device.** The app needs no backend of ours.
- **You bring your own AI key.** You connect an AI provider during setup. The tutor uses it to design your program, talk with you, and grade your speech. Your provider may charge for this.
- **Daily study doesn't need AI.** Once your program is designed, its exercises and answers are already prepared.
- **Language packs are open source.** Each language ships as a pack that you can install and update.
